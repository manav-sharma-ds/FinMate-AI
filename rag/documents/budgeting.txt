Budgeting is the process of planning how income will be used for expenses, savings, and financial goals.

A monthly budget helps track income and spending. A person can compare planned expenses with actual expenses to identify areas where spending can be reduced.

Common budget categories include food, housing, transportation, utilities, shopping, entertainment, healthcare, and education.

A useful budgeting approach is to prioritize essential expenses first, then savings and financial goals, followed by discretionary spending.
